From Forest to Finish

How your millwork is born.

A disciplined four-phase guild workflow guaranteeing zero unexpected delays or misalignment.

01

3D Laser Scanning

We map wall plumbness, floor levels, and plumbing penetrations with sub-millimeter Leica scanners before drawing CAD blueprints.

02

Lumber Selection

You are invited to our lumber yard to hand-select walnut or white oak flitches to approve grain characteristics and figure.

03

Workshop Joinery

Dovetailing, tenoning, and edge-banding in our local shop. Entire kitchens are pre-assembled on our level shop floor to test fit.

04

White-Glove Install

Installed strictly by our own full-time artisans—never subcontracted out. Laser scribed to walls with museum-level flush reveals.

Frequently Asked Questions

Semi-custom cabinets are pre-manufactured in 3-inch increments, using particleboard or MDF cores and filling leftover gaps with ugly filler strips. Atelier Ironwood cabinets are built completely from scratch in 1/16th-inch tolerances with 100% solid hardwood face frames and 3/4" furniture-grade plywood or solid core boxes. Zero filler strips; total architectural integration.
Once architectural CAD shop drawings and physical wood stain samples are approved, fabrication takes 6 to 9 weeks in our local studio. Because we build locally in California, you never face months of ocean freight delays or lost container shipments.
We exclusively use kiln-dried hardwoods with strict 6–8% moisture content and acclimatize all raw lumber inside our climate-controlled workshop for 3 weeks prior to milling. For wide slab doors, we integrate internal steel stiffener bars and expansion slots that allow natural seasonal micro-breathing without twisting.
